About this projection

This is a scenario, not a forecast consensus. Physical-AI takeoff aligned with Musk / Adcock: 1 billion humanoids by 2036 — not Morgan Stanley's 2050 case.

Humans follow UN WPP 2024 medium variant (peak ~10.3B around 2084).

Unemployment is a 20th-century statistic under labor substitution. After peak displacement it falls because the measure becomes meaningless — most of the "unemployed" are not looking.

World GDP is real 2026 USD, compounded from 24/7 robot productivity on top of a slowly growing human baseline.
